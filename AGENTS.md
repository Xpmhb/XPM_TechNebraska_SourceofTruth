## 4. Brand Voice & Anti-Slop Copywriting Rules

Tech Nebraska sounds like an authentic, pragmatic Midwestern enterprise leader. It avoids coastal startup hyperbole and academic jargon.

### The 4 Tone Dimensions
- **Authoritative & Legitimate:** Backed by hard numbers, legislative policy, and Fortune 500 enterprise participation.
- **Pragmatic Midwestern Grit:** Grounded in physical industry (freight, agriculture, insurance) and real ROI.
- **Collaborative & Inclusive:** Statewide representation (urban Omaha to rural panhandle).
- **Measured Urgency:** Forward-leaning without catastrophizing.

### ❌ Strictly Banned AI Buzzwords (Anti-Slop Standard)
Never use these words in Tech Nebraska copy:
*delve, tapestry, beacon, testament, paramount, pivotal, nestled, bustling, symphony, game-changer, revolutionary, supercharge, seamless, unlock, foster, elevate (unless referencing the official pillar 'Elevating the Good Life').*
