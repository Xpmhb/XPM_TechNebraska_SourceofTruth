The "Link-Dump" Reach Suppression Penalty

Algorithm audit revealing why nearly half of current posts experience depressed reach.

Link-in-Post Suppression 48% Affected

48.0% of LinkedIn posts and 63.6% of Facebook posts dump bare outbound links directly in the caption (Mailchimp, Zoom). LinkedIn algorithms penalize outbound links by up to 60%.

Performance Comparison +441% Lift

Outbound link posts average 12 median reactions. Native multi-photo posts and document carousels average 65+ median reactions.

The New Protocol Best Practice

Deliver the complete takeaway natively in the post body; place destination links in the first comment or a pinned follow-up reply.

Visual Hierarchy Approved Rule

One primary takeaway per section or card. Lead with the human quote or key metric; put evidence and attribution directly beneath.

Color Discipline Approved Rule

Neutral surfaces (white, obsidian, soft mist) dominate 80% of layout. Reserve vibrant Blue, Cyan, and Gold for emphasis and key hierarchy.

Authentic Media Standard Approved Rule

All social and editorial graphics must feature actual photography of Nebraska builders, workplaces, campus labs, and Summit moments.

Typographic Consistency Approved Rule

Core Creative Principles

Four governing rules that keep all content recognizable, elevated, and engaging without feeling repetitive.

01 · Photo and Video First

Lead with actual builders, enterprise workplaces, research labs, and the authentic texture of Nebraska’s growing technology ecosystem.

02 · One Visual Signature

Use subtle brand color collisions and fine grain as an intentional framing accent, never as distracting background clutter.

03 · Grounded & Mostly Neutral

Anchor layouts in crisp white, obsidian, and soft mist. Use Tech Blue and Goldenrod selectively for focus, badges, and primary action.

04 · Business-First Voice

Substantive, pragmatic, statewide, and confident. Human warmth and personality support institutional credibility rather than replacing it.
