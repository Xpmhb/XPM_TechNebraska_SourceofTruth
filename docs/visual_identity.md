# Tech Nebraska: Visual Identity & Design System Standards

## 1. The 80/20 Color Ratio Rule (Emspace Brand Guide P.20)
- **80% Neutral Canvas:** Obsidian Black (`#241F20`) for dark mode canvases or Off-White (`#D2E4F2`) for light backgrounds.
- **20% Vibrant Brand Accent:** Electric Tech Blue (`#0080C7`), Nebraska Goldenrod (`#FFB900`), and Ice Cyan (`#67D2DF`).
- **Never:** Flood layouts with solid bright color backgrounds. Keep the foundation grounded, allowing color to pop on buttons, badges, metrics, and vector marks.

## 2. Grainy Orb Background Gradients (Page 25)
- The brand backgrounds feature textured, grainy collisions of Electric Blue, Goldenrod, and Ice Cyan over Obsidian Black.
- Concept: Represents "colors colliding to symbolize community within the tech sector."
- Use rendered 4K backgrounds located in `assets/backgrounds/rendered_4k/`.

## 3. Typography Standards
- **Display Headings & Technical Metrics:** `Space Grotesk` (Geometric, monospaced tech feel).
- **Body Text & Interface Elements:** `Work Sans` (Clean, legible, humanistic sans-serif).

## 4. Logo Clear Space & Rules
- Always preserve clear space equal to the height of the state icon mark around the wordmark.
- Never stretch, skew, or recolor the master logos outside the approved source of truth files.
