# Tech Nebraska: Brand Voice, Tone & Anti-Slop Copywriting Standards

## 1. Tone Spectrum & Dimensions

```
Authoritative  ←――――――――[●]――――――――→ Academic
Pragmatic Grit ←――――――――[●]――――――――→ Hyped-Up Tech Hype
Community-Led  ←――――――――[●]――――――――→ Insular Corporate
Measured Speed ←――――――――[●]――――――――→ Alarmist Urgency
```

## 2. Voice Chart: Do's vs. Don'ts

| Dimension | How Tech Nebraska Sounds (DO) | What Tech Nebraska NEVER Says (DON'T) |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority** | "BIA funding expanded to $15M for FY26–27 to fuel startup scale." | "We are super pumped to revolutionize Midwest VC synergies!" |
| **Pragmatism** | "Where prairie endurance meets enterprise AI systems." | "Enter the boundless digital metaverse of infinite possibilities." |
| **Community** | "When Nebraska leaders put their minds together, our state leads." | "Tech Nebraska is single-handedly rescuing our backward tech scene." |
| **Urgency** | "We don't just participate in the digital future—we build it." | "Nebraska is desperately falling behind the coasts and dying!" |

## 3. Banned AI Slop Dictionary

Agents generating copy for Tech Nebraska must reject generic AI vocabulary:
- ❌ *Delve, tapestry, beacon, testament, paramount, pivotal, nestled, bustling, symphony, game-changer, revolutionary, supercharge, seamless, unlock, foster.*
- ✅ *Build, convene, invest, scale, deploy, modernize, legislate, fund, partner, anchor, grit.*
