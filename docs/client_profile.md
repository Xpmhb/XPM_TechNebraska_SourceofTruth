## Legislative & Economic Programs
- **Business Innovation Act (BIA):** State matching grant program championed by Tech Nebraska, expanded to $15 Million for FY26–27 to support R&D, prototype grants, and seed funding for startups.
- **Annual Tech Nebraska Summit:** The state's premier technology convention featuring 500+ attendees, executive roundtables, startup showcases, and legislative briefings.
