Project Overview
The 2026 Tech Nebraska Summit will launch a six-month content program. XPM will work alongside Tech 
Nebraska’s team to capture priority people and ideas, then turn them into consistent content through April 2027.
The goal: build a high-value content reserve that powers a consistent statewide editorial program, supports 
membership growth, and makes ongoing communications easier to manage and measure.
What's Included
Strategy + Summit Content Engine
Run a lightweight System’s Check of current content, social, website/SEO, analytics, and measurement; establish 
kickoff benchmarks.
Align on goals, audiences, policy guardrails, KPIs, approvals, and team roles.
Set six-month content pillars, recurring series, regional rotation, platform roles, and cadence.
Audit ticket holders and prioritize speakers, board members, sponsors, builders, and ecosystem leaders for 
interviews.
Build the shoot gameplan: interview questions, shot list, B-roll/sizzle direction, run of show, asset plan, and 
approvals.
Coordinate with the AV partner so XPM capture complements full-session recording.
Summit Production + Immediate Post
Oct. 20 VIP Mixer: two-person XPM team captures scheduled interviews and light photo/video content.
Oct. 21 Summit: three-person XPM team captures interviews, photography, social-first video, and B-roll while 
the AV partner records full sessions.
Deliver 1–2 strong photos per priority stakeholder, organized by person, with day-of/next-day selects where 
possible.
Publish Emily’s thank-you video Oct. 22 and the Summit recap/sizzle target of Oct. 23, tagging key sponsors and 
people.
Organize photos, session recordings, footage, and interviews into a shared content library + six-month 
repurposing map.
6-Month Content Growth Program
XPM will turn the Summit library, existing assets, and ecosystem activity into a six-month program centered on 
Nebraska members, builders, businesses, and practitioners. XPM leads strategy, production, website execution, and 
publishing support alongside Tech Nebraska’s team, which retains organizational context, voice, and final approval.
14–16 social pieces/month, developed LinkedIn-first and adapted to Facebook and Instagram, including 6–8 short-
form videos; exact mix flexes by strategy, source material, website priorities, and performance.
Up to 2 blogs/articles per month; format, SEO emphasis, and editorial mix are finalized with Tech Nebraska 
during strategy and guided by performance, source material, and priorities.
Website management: publishing, page updates, on-page SEO, metadata, internal linking, and priority keyword 
improvements.
Recurring editorial lanes: member/builder stories, Technology in Practice/AI, Nebraska tech intelligence, 
ecosystem/opportunity, and Tech Nebraska/membership; final series themes are set with the team.
5–8 Speaker + Partner Amplification Kits with approved assets, suggested copy, tags, and share guidance.
2–3 ready-to-post executive/board advocacy options per month.
Monthly KPI scorecard prioritizing membership growth, quality engagement, member/partner amplification, 
geographic representation, and audience growth using a Create → Publish → Measure → Learn → Adjust loop.
