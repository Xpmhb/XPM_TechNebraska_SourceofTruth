## 9\. Key Insights for Content Strategy
### What the Data Says
1. **People > Logos.** The top-performing content across every platform features individual human faces and names, not branded graphics. Laurel's portrait (46 eng), Emily's announcement (33 eng), and Advisory Board spotlights (65-91 eng) all prove this.
2. **Carousels and Video crush static images.** On Instagram, Reels got +137.5% and carousels got +125% lift over flat graphics. On LinkedIn, multi-photo carousels hit 161 engagement vs 12 for link dumps. Yet 75.7% of Instagram was static and 48% of LinkedIn was link drops.
3. **Tag people, tag companies, tag everyone.** The collaboration lift is +65.9% on LinkedIn when executives and sponsors are tagged. Untagged posts die.
4. **Stop dropping naked links.** External link posts are algorithmically suppressed. Newsletter content should be reformatted as native carousels with key takeaways, not Mailchimp URLs.
5. **Instagram is the event engine** and it's been off for 8.5 months during the most important Summit cycle yet. In 2025, 51.4% of the entire feed was Summit content. In 2026: zero.
6. **Zero short-form video in 2026.** Across all channels. The YouTube archive alone has 10+ hours of reusable executive content.
7. **The paid social field is empty.** No Nebraska tech association runs Meta ads. Even $500/mo in hyper-targeted ads would give Tech NE uncontested reach.
8. **The keyword landscape is wide open.** Nearly every target keyword has 0% difficulty. The issue is missing pages (no member directory, no policy hub), not competition.
9. **Backlinks are dangerously concentrated.** 71% from one domain ([nechamber.com](http://nechamber.com)). The 15 corporate members represent massive untapped authority but zero current links.
10. **Comment engagement is weak** (1.0 per post avg) because posts don't ask questions or invite conversation. The audience quality is high, they just aren't being prompted.
