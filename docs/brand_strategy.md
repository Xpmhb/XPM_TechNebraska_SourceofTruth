## Official Corebook Brand Language (preserve verbatim)

### Purpose statement
Tech Nebraska connects and strengthens Nebraska’s technology community by driving industry growth to position the state as a leader in the future of technology and innovation, advocating for tech-forward public policies and developing a diverse, talented tech ecosystem.

### Mission
Cultivating Nebraska’s tech community to drive collaboration, growth and policy.

### To achieve this mission, we will:
- Convene technology leaders to create an agenda that is pro-growth, pro-technology and pro-business
- Create statewide partnerships and efforts that advance organizational goals
- Influence legislation on federal, state and local levels to foster an environment that creates new technology businesses and jobs

### Vision
Nebraska and its economy are thriving and growing because of the innovation, diversity and leadership driven by a connected technology sector in the state.

### Tagline
Connecting people.

Advancing technology.

Elevating the good life.

These are the **Corebook's official words**; do not substitute XPM's framing for these statements. Source: https://my.corebook.io/tech-nebraska (client-supplied Brand Language sections, 2026-11-18).

## XPM 7-phase strategy (working interpretation; not official Corebook copy)

### Phase 1 — Brand Truth
- **Historical origin in previous XPM strategy:** Formed in 2023 in strategic alliance with the Nebraska Chamber of Commerce & Industry. Verify current legal/operating references before using externally.
- **XPM positioning interpretation:** Nebraska's industrial backbone (rail, freight, agriculture, insurance) and technology modernization can be told together. Statewide representation and credible, measured technology narratives are important editorial principles.

### Phase 2 — Audience Architecture (working segments)
1. Enterprise CIO/CTO and technology leaders — trusted peers, governance, talent and modernization.
2. High-growth technology founders — customer insight, investment, pilots and startup ecosystem access.
3. Civic leaders and policymakers — economic diversification, technology industry and workforce policy.

These are strategic target segments; examples of specific organizations are not assertions of current Tech Nebraska membership.

### Phase 3 — Cultural Positioning
**Previous XPM framing:** “Prairie Tech” and “Pragmatic Innovation”: show real-world business applications of technology rather than speculative hype. This is a creative positioning recommendation, **not Corebook-approved tagline or mission language**.

### Phase 4 — Three Guiding Pillars
The **official names** are:
1. **Connecting people.**
2. **Advancing technology.**
3. **Elevating the good life.**

The previous XPM descriptions were proposed editorial interpretations: connecting industries and leaders; enabling modernization in Nebraska sectors; highlighting strong careers and quality of life. Their wording is not part of the official tagline.

### Phase 5 — StoryBrand (SB7) Messaging
See `docs/storybrand_framework.md` for the **XPM working SB7 framework**, not official Corebook wording.

### Phase 6 — Brand Identity System
See `docs/visual_identity.md` for the **Corebook-aligned nine-color palette, official typography hierarchy, gradient combinations, logo usage, and contrast cautions**; consult `tokens.json` only after checking any stale tokens against these primary rules.

### Phase 7 — Go-To-Market & Ecosystem Governance
Historically proposed editorial opportunities include the annual Summit, regional Tech Talks/microsummits, membership growth and policy communication. Specific participation totals, funding figures, legislative positions, timings or recurring event schedules **require current source verification and appropriate approval before public use**.
